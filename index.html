Comic Studio

Canvas

  • Double-click empty space to open a text creator; press Enter to materialize the ring.
  • Drag on empty background to marquee-select items (and creators).
  • Shift-drag or two-finger scroll to pan. Pinch to zoom.

Items

  • Click to select · Drag to move · corner handles resize.
  • Double-click any item to edit its text individually.
  • Tab / ⇧Tab cycles fill colour · ← ↑ → ↓ cycles font on the selected item.
  • ← / → with nothing selected swaps the whole style bank.

Clipboard

  • Clicking any item copies it to the clipboard as a PNG. So does cycling colour/font, resizing, or committing a text edit.
  • ⌘C / Ctrl-C also copies the currently selected item.
  • ⌘V pastes image files onto the canvas.

Delete & reset

  • Delete / Backspace removes every selected item + creator.
  • Space × 2 pans to a fresh page (old work stays scrollable up).
  • Space × 3 wipes the whole board.
