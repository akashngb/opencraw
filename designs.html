Pick a layout

Every layout is a starting mesh — you can drag dividers, resize rows, add more rows, and split panels once you're inside.

Comic Designs

Canvas

  • Double-click anywhere to open a text creator; press Enter to place.
  • Paste (⌘V) images to drop them onto the stage.
  • Drag panel dividers to resize the mesh.

Bubbles

  • Click a style chip in the sidebar to add that bubble to the stage.
  • Double-click any placed bubble to edit its text.
  • Drag to move; corner handles resize once selected.

Save & export

  • Save project.json downloads the whole board; Load restores it.
  • Slice to A4 / A3 tiles the tall board into printable PDF pages.
  • Wipe text · keep images clears all text bubbles but leaves image panels.
